FAQ

What do I get?

An instant high-resolution digital file (1536×2304 JPG, print-grade) of the poster you buy. No shipping — it's yours immediately after payment.

Can I print it?

Yes — print at home, at any print shop, or use an online print service. The resolution is sized for up to A2 (16×24 in) at 96 dpi, larger with good print software.

Are these real streets?

Yes. Every poster is generated from real OpenStreetMap street data of the actual city — not a generic illustration.
